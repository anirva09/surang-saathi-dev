# Future Capabilities — Non-Binding Backlog

This file condenses useful capability ideas from the older comprehensive/component/website reports. It is a backlog, not an MVP checklist.

## Field and safety

- statutory checklist library/versioning
- equipment/machinery logs
- shift handover
- attendance
- richer near-miss patterns
- environmental sensor dashboards
- training/certification records

## Compliance and management

- configurable statutory rules
- escalation management
- cross-mine scorecards
- resource allocation planning
- customizable reports
- regulatory-impact analysis

## Contractor governance

- contractor documents
- safety certification status
- violation history
- wage-compliance evidence where legally/institutionally appropriate
- cross-mine trust score and blacklist flags with due-process controls

## Corporate / ministry

- national aggregate KPIs
- subsidiary comparisons
- audit dossier access
- GIS heatmaps
- policy analytics
- trend reporting

## AI/document intelligence

- OCR review queue
- multilingual speech-to-text
- on-device triage suggestion
- sensor anomaly detection
- predictive equipment/safety correlation

## Alternative channels

- SMS high-severity fallback where technically/operationally feasible
- WhatsApp reporting integrated into the same canonical data model

## Visualization

- 2D mine-section overlays
- geospatial hazard/inspection layers

Full 3D remains explicitly excluded unless a later operational use case demonstrates value.
